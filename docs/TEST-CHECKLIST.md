# Kevy does Devy: test checklist

A short run-through before handing it over. It needs about 20 minutes with a bass, and ideally the Anagram.

## Load

- [ ] The VST3 loads in Reaper or Ableton with nothing else installed, and the panel opens at a comfortable size.
- [ ] Drag the corner to resize: everything scales and stays readable.
- [ ] The skull blinks while idle.

## Delay A and Delay B

- [ ] Step through all 9 types on each delay. Each sounds different, with no clicks or dropouts.
- [ ] Feedback at maximum self-oscillates and stays controlled, with no sub rumble.
- [ ] Low Cut at 120 Hz keeps the repeats clear of the low end. At 20 Hz you get full-range repeats.
- [ ] Duck at 100%: the repeats dip while you play and swell back in the gaps.
- [ ] Right-click Tone and choose high-pass: the repeats thin out.
- [ ] Set a subdivision (1/8.) and tap the master tempo: the time follows. Sync to DAW tempo follows the project.
- [ ] HOLD INFINITE: the repeats sustain and new notes stay dry over them. Switch it off and the repeats decay.
- [ ] Feedback at maximum until it howls, then KILL TAILS: silence within a moment, infinite and freeze switch off, and the dry bass and looper carry on.
- [ ] Tap a tempo: each delay's ON light flashes at its time, and the TAP button pulses with the tempo.
- [ ] Fuzzy on Delay A: the repeats are clearly fuzzed, and still die away.
- [ ] MK I versus MK II: MK I is grittier and narrower, and the pitch types (Rainbow, Crystal) sound warblier.
- [ ] The skull's jaw chatters with the repeats.

## Reverb

- [ ] All 9 types, including Spring, Reverse, Filter and Dist-Verb.
- [ ] Shimmer adds an octave-up tail with no low-end mud.
- [ ] HOLD FREEZE holds the tail indefinitely. The skull gets icicles and says BRRRR.
- [ ] Trails on: switching a section off lets the tail ring out. Trails off cuts it.
- [ ] Change type with Merge on: the tail carries through the change.
- [ ] The skull's eyes roll with the reverb tail.

## Routing and extras

- [ ] All four routings work, and the panel label matches what you hear.
- [ ] BYPASS ALL leaves the dry sound, and the skull pulls a face (tongue out, BLEH).
- [ ] Kill Dry leaves only the effects.
- [ ] Global EQ changes the effects only. EQ on dry too changes everything.
- [ ] Presets: load a few, save one, reopen the project, and the saved preset is still there.

## Looper

- [ ] REC, then PLAY: the loop plays back seamlessly with no click at the loop point.
- [ ] DUB, then PLAY, then UNDO removes the overdub. UNDO again brings it back.
- [ ] HALF drops an octave and stays clean (sub guard). REV plays backwards.
- [ ] STOP, then PLAY restarts from the top. CLEAR empties it.
- [ ] PRE, MID and POST: with PRE, the effects process the loop.

## Footswitches with the Anagram

- [ ] Setup page, LAST MESSAGE: shows each switch as you press it, and which switch it maps to.
- [ ] C+D on a four-switch pedal kills the tails.
- [ ] The setup page says Connected: (Anagram), with Reaper's Anagram MIDI input disabled.
- [ ] In Stomp mode, A, B and C light up the badges. If not, set up the scene messages from the guide and try again.
- [ ] PLAY mode: A, B and C switch Delay A, Delay B and Reverb. A double-tap latches infinite or freeze, and another double-tap releases it.
- [ ] A+B, B+C and A+C switch modes, if the Anagram can send two at once.
- [ ] TAP mode: taps set the delay times and the master tempo.
- [ ] LOOPER mode: C records, plays and dubs, a double-tap stops, A reverses, and B halves the speed.
- [ ] PATCH mode: A and B step through presets.
- [ ] TEACH MY PEDAL learns A, B and C in turn, then the switches work.
- [ ] Every footswitch job also works from its on-screen button.

## Expression

The Anagram's Exp jack, or any MIDI expression on CC 11.

- [ ] Sweeping it moves Feedback A+B, and the bar moves.
- [ ] CALIBRATE fixes a pedal that reads backwards or only covers part of its range.
- [ ] Pick another target from the menu: the new target follows.
- [ ] Right-click a knob, choose MIDI Learn, move a controller: the knob follows it.
